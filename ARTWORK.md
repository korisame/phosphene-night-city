# Wallpaper artwork and provenance

Night City is a **fan-art project** inspired by Cyberpunk: Edgerunners. The source
illustrations were generated with AI assistance for this project. Procedural
animation adds city lighting, signs, weather, bridge traffic, lifts, rare aerial
crossings, character and clothing movement, and reflections. It does not use an
extracted anime episode, game capture or commercial soundtrack. The clips are silent.

The material is shared as a community desktop-wallpaper project. The repository's
MIT software license applies to its code; it is **not a blanket license to the
artwork or the underlying Cyberpunk characters, world and trademarks**. Those
third-party rights remain with their respective owners. No commercial-use rights
to those properties are granted here, and no affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

Current release: **v7 · Blue district**, originally authored as internal draft
v22. It is the existing approved 3840×2160,30fps,128-second master, not a new
upscale or rerender for publication. SHA-256:

```
a4f0335eae5f44793743800dd62801714e47c28a0698157b69b6ea48420fbde0
```

The website's24-second1080p preview is an excerpt of that master. The4K WebP still
is derived from the actual rendered scene. Detail images are crops from that
scene. The native app screenshots are isolated window captures; no personal
files, account data or whole-desktop screenshot is included. Screenshot conversion
removes embedded metadata. The website does not require external analytics or
remote fonts; GitHub's hosting infrastructure has its own service policies.

See [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) for authors and inspiration, and the release assets'
checksums for file identity. Earlier versions are retained as labelled history;
v6 includes the former train effect and is not the recommended current release.
