# Phosphene · Night City Edition

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Native macOS application; static web showcase and documentation.

## Stack

Existing Rust/AppKit controller with Swift platform services and wallpaper extension. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the public showcase; GitHub Pages keeps the site alongside its source repository.

## Users and purpose

People who want an animated cyberpunk desktop on macOS, and developers who want to inspect this community adaptation of Phosphene. The user commissioned a public, visually curated repository and live landing page including the created wallpapers, screenshots, a short video and author credits.

## Capabilities and constraints

A 3840 × 2160, 30 fps, 128-second fixed-camera Night City wallpaper is installed and verified on two displays. Phosphene 1.9.1 adds stable wallpaper revisions, History and native Apply Wallpaper. Rust owns the controller UI and computation; Apple integration remains Swift. Uses private macOS frameworks. Intel compilation was checked; runtime evidence is Apple Silicon. This is an independent community edition, not the upstream author's official release. No public signing/notarization claim.

## Brand commitments

Preserve the Phosphene name and approved icon with upstream attribution. Showcase the existing night city artwork and its dark OLED-oriented palette. No personal files, account names, private paths, notifications, widgets or signing identity in public screenshots.

## Evidence on hand

The current wallpaper master and its source layers/animation implementation; native app-window captures; local playback acceptance and tests. No testimonials, broad compatibility certification or universal performance benchmarks.

## Principles

Show the real work. Make download, version, platform requirements and provenance easy to find. Keep artwork, app source and third-party intellectual property credits distinct. Respect reduced motion and user-controlled playback.
